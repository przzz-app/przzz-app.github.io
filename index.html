Sorted the way you work.

PRzzz groups your pull requests the way GitHub's inbox does: what needs you comes first, then what's waiting on someone else, then what's done. Each section has its own colour, so you can tell them apart at a glance. You can drag the sections into any order.

Your review was dismissedNew commits reset your approval, and nobody asked you again.

Small app. Sharp details.

PRzzz stays out of your way until something needs you.

CI progress with real estimates

See how far along each build is, for example "Running · 3m of ~7m". Estimates come from the median of your last ten successful runs. When the checks finish, your inbox refreshes.

A badge that means something

The menu bar counts your review requests and your PRs that need action. The icon turns red when something of yours is blocked.

While you were away

Lock your Mac for lunch. When you come back, PRzzz shows what changed in a section of its own for the next ten minutes.

Pop out a floating window

Pin the PRs you're watching to a small panel that stays above your other apps and keeps itself up to date.

Filter by repository

Pick repositories, or use patterns like acme/web-*, so repositories created later match too. Your own PRs can always show.

Custom buckets

Make a section for the teammates whose work you follow, and see their open PRs in one place.

Your code stays yours.

PRzzz talks to GitHub's API directly from your Mac. There's no PRzzz server in between.

Token in the Keychain

Your token is stored in the macOS Keychain, and it's only ever sent to GitHub.

Sandboxed

PRzzz runs in the App Sandbox with network access only. It can't read your files.

Easy sign-in

Use the token GitHub CLI already has, or create a personal access token with the scopes filled in for you.

Light on your rate limit

Built-in rate limit stats show exactly how many calls PRzzz makes.
